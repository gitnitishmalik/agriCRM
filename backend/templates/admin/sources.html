What the approved sources are for

Doc 05 settles the scraping question. Institutional data is fair game — MCA director names and DINs published by statute, SFAC/NABARD lists, ISMA/NFCSF directories. Aggregate data is fair game and gives targeting without holding one unconsented number. Volume comes from partnership ingestion: one FPO MoU with a consent clause is roughly 1,200 clean records.

🔴 Personal data enters only via partner_agreement, field_collection, inbound_signup, or an approved theta_analytics / purchased_licensed batch (R4). A scraped list of a million is worth less than a consented list of fifty thousand.
